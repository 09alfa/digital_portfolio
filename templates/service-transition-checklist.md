# Service Transition Checklist

Use when moving a new product or service from delivery into live support.

**Service:** `<name>`  **Transition owner:** `<name>`  **Target go-live:** `<date>`

## 1. Service definition

- [ ] Service specification approved: scope, inclusions, exclusions, deployment model
- [ ] Responsibilities agreed between provider, customer and suppliers (RACI)
- [ ] Service levels and reporting defined
- [ ] Pricing and billing set up

## 2. Acceptance

- [ ] Acceptance criteria agreed with the customer before testing
- [ ] UAT completed and signed off
- [ ] Open defects reviewed, with workarounds documented

## 3. Operational readiness

- [ ] Runbooks and support procedures written and walked through
- [ ] Monitoring, alerting and dashboards live
- [ ] Incident, problem and change processes connected
- [ ] Escalation routes, including suppliers, published
- [ ] Access, licences and tooling in place for support teams

## 4. Knowledge transfer

- [ ] Support team trained and confident to operate the service
- [ ] Knowledge base articles published
- [ ] Customer administrators trained where needed

## 5. Sales and customer handover

- [ ] Sales handover covers what was promised to the customer
- [ ] Customer onboarding steps reviewed; any step that adds no value removed
- [ ] Customer contacts and communication plan agreed

## 6. Early life support

- [ ] Early life support period, team and exit criteria agreed
- [ ] Daily check-ins scheduled for the first week
- [ ] Exit review booked

## 7. Close

- [ ] Lessons learned captured
- [ ] Benefits tracking handed to the service owner
- [ ] Project closed and documentation archived
