# Backlog Prioritisation: RICE and MoSCoW

## RICE scoring

**Score = (Reach x Impact x Confidence) / Effort**

| Factor | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Customers or events affected per period | Number |
| Impact | Effect on the goal for each customer | 3 massive, 2 high, 1 medium, 0.5 low, 0.25 minimal |
| Confidence | How sure we are of the estimates | 100% high, 80% medium, 50% low |
| Effort | Team effort in person-weeks or sprints | Number |

## Adjust before you commit

RICE ranks; it does not decide. Before final ordering, check each item for:

- **Risk:** does it reduce a known operational, security or compliance risk?
- **Readiness:** can operations and support take it on?
- **Dependencies:** is something else blocking it?
- **Strategic fit:** does it serve this period's objectives?
