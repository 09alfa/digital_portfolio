# D03 | Core Banking Process Automation

> Delivered a first-of-its-kind integration under a fixed deadline and through COVID-19, with no executive escalation and an approach reused at two more banks.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Listed West African IT services group |
| **My role** | Engagement lead |
| **Period** | Within 2015 to 2023 |
| **Customer** | Multinational bank with 20,000+ staff across four continents |
| **Team** | Seven: four developers, two operations, one shadow |
| **Relevant for** | Project Manager, Delivery Manager, Technical Account Manager, Business Analyst |

## Context

The bank wanted to automate front-end processes by integrating a business process management (BPM) platform with its core banking system, to speed up customer acquisition, improve service and end reporting non-compliance.

## Challenge

- No reference integration existed between the two platforms
- Scope and effort were unknown at the start
- Success had to be defined iteratively, against a fixed deadline
- Delivery ran through COVID-19, with illness affecting the team

## What I did

- Defined requirements with the customer and led business process documentation
- Ran sprint reviews and workshops to agree each increment
- Managed customer stakeholders and vendors, and negotiated rates and level of effort
- Worked with the PMO on resourcing
- Built a goal and impact dashboard with key stakeholders
- Set a clear issue path: simulate in UAT, classify as product bug or known issue, apply a workaround, then hand to the vendor with steps, sample records and logs
- Moved the engagement from a resource-based to an outcome-based model through discussions with the bank's senior technology leaders
- Separated project and business-as-usual teams, with weekly impact checks

**Handling disruption:** when COVID-19 hit the team, I worked with the PMO to reschedule, reduced contact hours, added shadow staff and put health provisions in place. Where stakeholders went quiet, I worked through allies inside the bank rather than escalating.

## Results

- **50+** customisations and **20** APIs delivered
- **No escalation** to executive sponsors
- Approach reused at two further financial institutions in record time
- **US$0.87m** revenue across three banks from this and related transformation work
- Implementation-partner status with two technology vendors

## Skills demonstrated

Delivery under uncertainty; stakeholder and vendor management; commercial negotiation; change enablement; turning a project into a reusable approach.

## Methods and tools

Scrum ceremonies, BPM documentation, impact dashboards, UAT triage, PMO resourcing.
