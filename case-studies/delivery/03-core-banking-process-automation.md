# D03 | Core Banking Process Automation

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Listed West African IT services group |
| **My role** | Engagement lead |
| **Customer** | Multinational bank with 20,000+ staff and operations across four continents |
| **Team** | Seven: four developers, two operations, one shadow |
| **Focus** | Integration delivery under uncertainty, stakeholder and vendor management |

## Context

The bank wanted to automate front-end processes by integrating a business process management (BPM) platform with its core banking system. The goals were faster customer acquisition, better service interaction and an end to reporting non-compliance.

## Why it was hard

- No reference integration existed between the two platforms
- Scope and effort were unknown at the start
- Success had to be defined iteratively, against a fixed customer deadline
- Delivery ran through COVID-19, with staff illness affecting the team

## What I did

- Defined requirements with the customer and led business process documentation
- Ran sprint reviews and organised workshops to agree each increment
- Managed customer stakeholders and vendors, and negotiated rates and level of effort
- Worked with the PMO on resourcing
- Built a goal and impact dashboard with key stakeholders
- Set up a clear issue path: simulate in UAT, classify as product bug or known issue, apply a workaround, then hand over to the vendor with steps, sample records and logs
- Moved the engagement from a resource-based to an outcome-based model through discussions with the bank's senior technology leaders
- Separated project and business-as-usual teams, with weekly impact checks

## Handling disruption

When COVID-19 affected the team, I worked with the PMO to reschedule, reduced contact hours, added shadow staff and put health provisions in place. Where stakeholders went quiet, I worked through allies inside the bank rather than escalating.

## Results

- **50+** customisations and **20** APIs delivered
- **No escalation** to executive sponsors
- The documented approach was reused at two further financial institutions in record time
- **US$0.87m** revenue across three banks from this and related transformation work
- Implementation-partner status gained with two technology vendors

## What this shows

- Delivering under real uncertainty with a fixed deadline
- Stakeholder, vendor and commercial management in one engagement
- Turning a one-off project into a reusable delivery approach
