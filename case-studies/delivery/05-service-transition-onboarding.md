# D05 | Service Transition and Customer Onboarding

> Made new services faster to hand over and faster for customers to use: 30% quicker transition and onboarding cut from days to hours.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Global technology reseller, systems integrator and Azure Expert MSP (UK) |
| **My role** | Product Manager, Cloud and Managed Services |
| **Period** | 2023 to 2024 |
| **Scope** | Six productisation initiatives run in parallel: hybrid cloud, SaaS security, backup and resilience, modern workspace, packaged professional services |
| **Relevant for** | Delivery Manager, Service Delivery Manager, Project Manager, Customer Success |

## Context

Services were being sold faster than operations could support them. Handover was slow, customers waited too long to see value, and some launches led to repeated critical escalations.

## Challenge

Close the gap between what was sold and what operations could run, without slowing sales.

## What I did

- **Wrote clear service specifications.** Each of the 20+ service categories got specifications, statements of work and handover artefacts covering responsibilities, support expectations and acceptance criteria.
- **Introduced readiness gates.** Go/no-go reviews before launch covered documentation, support ownership, supplier inputs and open risks.
- **Redesigned onboarding.** Journey mapping and stakeholder workshops showed where customers waited on steps that added no value.
- **Kept one view across six initiatives** with integrated schedules, RAID logs, dependency maps and leadership dashboards.
- **Governed suppliers** against milestones and agreed corrective actions where gaps appeared.
- **Stopped premature releases** when readiness evidence was missing, even under commercial pressure.

## Results

- **30%** faster service transition
- **11** redundant onboarding steps removed; time-to-value cut from **days to hours**
- Repeated critical post-launch escalations resolved
- OKR and KPI dashboards that sped up executive approvals

## Skills demonstrated

Service transition; operational readiness; release control; customer onboarding; supplier governance; matrix stakeholder management.
