# D04 | Multi-Country Infrastructure and Cloud Migrations

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Listed West African IT services group |
| **My roles** | Professional Services Engineer (2008 to 2013), then Delivery Manager (2013 to 2015) |

| **Focus** | Implementation, migration, cutover, service transition, incident reduction |

## Context

This is where my delivery foundation was built: first hands-on, implementing infrastructure for enterprise customers, then leading the teams that did it across several countries.

## As an engineer

- Delivered **50+** implementation, proof-of-concept and migration projects, **98%** on time
- Architected and led **10+** business-critical solution projects within that total
- Designed and implemented **11** storage area network and private cloud environments (VMware, NetApp)
- Migrated **5,000+** servers across **13** customer estates
- Held revenue budget accountability for the SAN and private cloud solution lines

## As Delivery Manager

- Led hybrid infrastructure and cloud migration teams across **four countries**
- Protected business continuity through readiness assessments, dependency mapping, controlled cutover and rollback planning
- Moved **five** enterprise customers into live service across managed services, core banking and infrastructure deployments, through acceptance, knowledge transfer and early life support
- Led a Level 2 escalation team of **four** engineers
- Cut repeat service incidents by **40%** through supplier performance reviews, root-cause analysis and incident governance

## Across the wider period

Contributed to 25 enterprise transformation projects, including 8 private and hybrid cloud migrations, 6 data centre migration and modernisation projects, 3 data migrations, 2 core banking replacements, 4 digital journey enablements and 2 customer acquisition process transformations.

## What this shows

- Real technical depth behind later product and delivery decisions
- Cutover, rollback and transition discipline
- Continuous improvement through incident and supplier management
