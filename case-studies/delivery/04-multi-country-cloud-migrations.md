# D04 | Multi-Country Infrastructure and Cloud Migrations

> From hands-on engineer to delivery manager: 5,000+ servers migrated, teams led in four countries, and repeat incidents cut by 40%.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Listed West African IT services group |
| **My roles** | Professional Services Engineer (2008 to 2013), Delivery Manager (2013 to 2015) |

## Context

This is where my delivery foundation was built: first implementing infrastructure for enterprise customers myself, then leading the teams that did it across several countries.

## Challenge

Move business-critical systems for banks, telecoms and other enterprises without disrupting the services their own customers relied on.

## What I did

**As an engineer**
- Delivered **50+** implementation, proof-of-concept and migration projects, **98%** on time
- Architected and led **10+** business-critical solution projects within that total
- Designed and implemented **11** storage area network and private cloud environments (VMware, NetApp)
- Migrated **5,000+** servers across **13** customer estates
- Held revenue budget accountability for the SAN and private cloud solution lines

**As Delivery Manager**
- Led hybrid infrastructure and cloud migration teams across **four countries**
- Protected continuity through readiness assessments, dependency mapping, controlled cutover and rollback planning
- Moved **five** enterprise customers into live service across managed services, core banking and infrastructure deployments
- Led a Level 2 escalation team of **four** engineers
- Ran supplier performance reviews and root-cause analysis on recurring faults

## Results

- Repeat service incidents down **40%**
- Five enterprise customers transitioned into stable live service
- Contributed to **25** enterprise transformation projects across the wider period: 8 private and hybrid cloud migrations, 6 data centre migration and modernisation projects, 4 digital journey enablements, 3 data migrations, 2 core banking replacements and 2 customer acquisition process transformations

## Skills demonstrated

Technical depth in infrastructure and cloud; migration and cutover; escalation management; supplier governance; continuous improvement.

## Methods and tools

VMware, NetApp, SAN, private cloud; [service transition checklist](../../templates/service-transition-checklist.md), incident and problem management.
