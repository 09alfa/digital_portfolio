# D01 | Banking Account Migration

> Moved 23,000 customer accounts to a new platform, protecting continuity through verification scripting, rehearsed cutover and tested rollback.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | UK product and technology consultancy |
| **My role** | Project lead |
| **Period** | 2024 to present |
| **Scale** | 23,000 customer accounts, regulated financial services |
| **Relevant for** | Project Manager, Technical Project Manager, Delivery Manager |

## Context

A financial-services client needed to move 23,000 customer accounts to a new platform. Customers could not lose access to their money or records.

## Challenge

Run a migration where any error would be visible immediately, without a long freeze on the business.

## What I did

- **Mapped dependencies** between data, systems, teams and third parties, and turned them into a sequenced plan with clear owners.
- **Built verification in from the start.** Verification scripting compared source and target records, so accuracy was checked by evidence rather than sampling alone.
- **Rehearsed the cutover** before the live event and used the rehearsal to tighten timings, sequence and roles.
- **Prepared rollback** with defined triggers, so a decision to stop or reverse could be made quickly.
- **Wrote operational runbooks** for the cutover window and early support.
- **Kept stakeholders informed** through one plan, a RAID log and agreed go/no-go checkpoints.

## Results

- **23,000** accounts migrated
- Service continuity protected through rehearsed cutover and rollback readiness
- Runbooks handed to support for early life

## Skills demonstrated

Migration planning; dependency management; cutover and rollback; business continuity; evidence-based go/no-go decisions.
