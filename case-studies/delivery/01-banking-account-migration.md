# D01 | Banking Account Migration

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | UK product and technology consultancy |
| **My role** | Project lead |
| **Scale** | 23,000 customer accounts |
| **Environment** | Regulated financial services |
| **Focus** | Migration planning, cutover, rollback, business continuity |

## Context

A financial-services client needed to move 23,000 customer accounts to a new platform. Customers could not lose access to their money or records, and any error would be visible immediately.

## What I did

- **Mapped dependencies** between data, systems, teams and third parties, and turned them into a sequenced plan with clear owners.
- **Built verification in from the start.** Verification scripting compared source and target records, so accuracy was checked by evidence rather than sampling alone.
- **Rehearsed the cutover** before the live event, using the rehearsal to tighten timings, sequence and roles.
- **Prepared rollback arrangements** with defined triggers, so the decision to stop or reverse could be made quickly.
- **Wrote operational runbooks** for the cutover window and for support after go-live.
- **Kept stakeholders informed** through a single plan, a RAID log and agreed go/no-go checkpoints.

## Results

- **23,000** accounts migrated
- Service continuity protected through rehearsed cutover and rollback readiness
- Operational runbooks handed to support for early life

## What this shows

- Running a high-risk migration in a regulated environment
- Cutover, rollback and business continuity planning
- Evidence-based go/no-go decisions
