# D06 | Multi-Country Service Expansion and Portfolio Governance

> Extended services into 23 countries, 19 without a local office, while running governance and finance across a multimillion-dollar portfolio.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Listed West African IT services group |
| **My role** | Senior Product Manager |
| **Period** | Within 2015 to 2023 |
| **Scale** | 23 countries; 15+ client engagements in parallel; multimillion-dollar portfolio |
| **People** | Six direct reports; matrix of 300+ engineering and support staff allocated to the portfolio |
| **Relevant for** | Senior Product Manager, Programme or Portfolio Manager, Delivery Manager, IT Manager |

## Context

The group had offices in four countries but customers and opportunities in many more.

## Challenge

Serve new markets without building a local office in each, and keep control of a growing portfolio.

## What I did

- **Managed the expansion strategy.** Extended services into 23 countries, four with offices and 19 without, using repeatable delivery and partner models.
- **Ran PMO-aligned portfolio governance** with integrated schedule, RAID, financial, dependency and benefit controls across 15+ client engagements.
- **Controlled finances:** budgets, forecasts, supplier quotations, purchase orders, work in progress, billing and margins.
- **Reported to executives** through C-suite and customer dashboards covering schedule, finance, suppliers, risks, incidents, changes and benefits.
- **Restructured service delivery** to simplify resource assignment and speed up deployment.
- **Established a Support Hub service model** for repeatable support across customers.
- **Developed people,** coaching technical colleagues into product responsibilities through roadmap, KPI and stakeholder coaching.

## Results

- **23** countries served, **19** without a local office
- **US$1.4m** generated from the expansion
- **US$0.6m** new services revenue in 2020, within a US$3m services line
- Clearer executive visibility across the portfolio

## Skills demonstrated

Portfolio and programme governance; financial control; executive reporting; expansion strategy; people leadership through a matrix and direct reports.

## Methods and tools

PMO governance, benefits tracking, [status report](../../templates/status-report.md), [RACI and stakeholder plan](../../templates/raci-and-stakeholder-plan.md).
