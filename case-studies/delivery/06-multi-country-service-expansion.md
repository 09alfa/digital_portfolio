# D06 | Multi-Country Service Expansion and Portfolio Governance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Listed West African IT services group |
| **My role** | Senior Product Manager |
| **Scale** | 23 countries; 15+ client engagements in parallel; multimillion-dollar portfolio |
| **People** | Six direct reports; matrix of 300+ engineering and support staff allocated to the portfolio |
| **Focus** | Expansion strategy, portfolio governance, financial control, executive reporting |

## Context

The group had offices in four countries but customers and opportunities in many more. Growth depended on serving new markets without building a local office in each.

## What I did

- **Managed the expansion strategy.** Extended services into 23 countries: four with local offices and 19 without, using repeatable delivery and partner models rather than bespoke country builds.
- **Ran PMO-aligned portfolio governance.** Integrated schedule, RAID, financial, dependency and benefit controls across 15+ client engagements.
- **Controlled finances.** Tracked budgets, forecasts, supplier quotations, purchase orders, work in progress, billing and margins so problems surfaced early.
- **Reported to executives.** Produced C-suite and customer executive dashboards covering schedule, finance, suppliers, risks, incidents, changes and benefits.
- **Restructured service delivery** to simplify how resources were assigned and speed up deployment.
- **Established a Support Hub service model** for repeatable support across customers.
- **Developed people.** Coached technical colleagues into product responsibilities through roadmap, KPI and stakeholder coaching.

## Results

- **23** countries served, **19** without a local office
- **US$1.4m** generated from the expansion
- **US$0.6m** new services revenue in 2020, within a US$3m services line
- Clearer executive visibility across a multimillion-dollar portfolio

## What this shows

- Portfolio and programme governance at scale
- Financial and commercial control alongside delivery
- Leading through a matrix as well as direct reports
