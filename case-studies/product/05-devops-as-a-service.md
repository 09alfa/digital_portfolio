# P05 | DevOps-as-a-Service Productisation

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | UK product and technology consultancy |
| **My role** | Product Manager (service design and productisation) |
| **Customer** | Regulated financial-services organisation |
| **Focus** | Turning technical capability into a repeatable service product |

## Context

The customer's releases were slow and carried risk, which mattered in a regulated environment. Rather than run a one-off engagement, the aim was to package the capability as a repeatable service.

## What I did

- **Defined the service scope:** CI/CD advisory, pipeline implementation support, release governance, monitoring, environment management, operating model design and transition into live service.
- **Set clear boundaries** between what the service team owned and what stayed with the customer, so support expectations were agreed before go-live.
- **Introduced automated release rehearsals** and explicit readiness criteria, so go/no-go decisions rested on evidence.
- **Governed the go-live** through readiness reviews covering test evidence, outstanding risks, support handover and early life support.

## Results

- **40%** reduction in time to go live
- A repeatable service proposition that can be offered to other customers
- Clear operating responsibilities agreed before release

## What this shows

- Productising a technical service rather than delivering it as bespoke work
- Release governance and operational readiness in a regulated setting
- Translating engineering practice into business outcomes

**Note:** this was a product and service design role, not a hands-on engineering role.
