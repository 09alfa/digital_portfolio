# P05 | DevOps-as-a-Service Productisation

> Packaged release and environment capability as a repeatable service for a regulated customer, cutting time to go live by 40%.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | UK product and technology consultancy |
| **My role** | Product Manager (service design and productisation) |
| **Period** | 2024 to present |
| **Customer** | Regulated financial-services organisation |
| **Relevant for** | Product Manager, Service Delivery Manager, Delivery Manager, Technical Account Manager |

## Context

The customer's releases were slow and risky, which mattered in a regulated environment.

## Challenge

Fix the immediate problem in a way that could be offered again, rather than as one-off consulting.

## What I did

- **Defined the service scope:** CI/CD advisory, pipeline implementation support, release governance, monitoring, environment management, operating model design and transition into live service.
- **Agreed clear boundaries** between what the service team owned and what stayed with the customer, before go-live.
- **Introduced automated release rehearsals** and explicit readiness criteria, so go/no-go decisions rested on evidence.
- **Governed go-live** through readiness reviews covering test evidence, outstanding risks, support handover and early life support.

## Results

- **40%** reduction in time to go live
- A repeatable service proposition for other customers
- Operating responsibilities agreed before release

## Skills demonstrated

Service design; productisation; release governance; operational readiness; translating engineering practice into business outcomes.

## Methods and tools

Release readiness gates, RACI, runbooks, early life support. This was a product and service design role, not a hands-on engineering role.
