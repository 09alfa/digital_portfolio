# P01 | IaaS Platform: From Idea to Recurring Revenue

> Built a consumption-based infrastructure platform, offered hosted or as private cloud on customer premises, that cut operating costs and opened a new recurring revenue line.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Listed West African IT services group with a Tier-3 data centre |
| **My role** | Senior Product Manager (initiated and owned the platform) |
| **Period** | Within 2015 to 2023 |
| **Customers** | Banks, insurers, oil and gas, manufacturing, SMBs and technology hubs |
| **Relevant for** | Product Manager, Senior Product Manager, Product and Proposition Manager, IT Manager |

## Context

Enterprise customers were paying heavily upfront for infrastructure that sat partly idle. Buying cycles were long, and regulated customers often could not move workloads to overseas public cloud because of data protection rules.

## Challenge

Give these customers cloud economics without asking them to give up control of where their data lives or to change how they buy technology, and turn that into a profitable service line.

## What I did

- **Validated demand before investing.** Ran discovery across sectors to confirm which workloads customers would move first: test and development, disaster recovery, backup and production.
- **Shaped the product.** Offered compute, storage, backup and disaster recovery as a service, plus partner PaaS and SaaS such as data archiving and analytics.
- **Gave customers a choice of hosting.** Capacity could be consumed from our data centre or run as dedicated private cloud on the customer's own premises, with data kept in-country either way.
- **Built a consumption pricing model.** Compute per virtual server (memory, vCPU and disk), storage per terabyte with a minimum commitment, basic administration as a percentage of hosting, specialist administration by service agreement, and revenue share with platform partners.
- **Took it to market.** Positioned the offer around moving from capital to operating spend, pay as you grow and cost visibility. Sold through proposals, customer references and webinars, with enablement for sales, pre-sales and technical teams.
- **Supported mixed estates.** Hosted Linux, open-source and commercial software stacks, so customers did not need to change application vendors.
- **Worked with security, compliance and legal** so the platform met data protection requirements and customer commitments.

## Results

- **19%** reduction in operating costs through platform modernisation
- A **new recurring revenue** stream for the business
- **70%+** reduction in technology-acquisition costs for four financial institutions
- A base for later IaaS and SaaS services (see [P04](04-xaas-platform-scale-reliability.md))

## Skills demonstrated

Product strategy and ownership from idea to in-life service; customer discovery; pricing and packaging; commercialising infrastructure; go-to-market; regulated environments.

## Methods and tools

Customer discovery, value proposition canvas, business cases, pricing models, go-to-market planning.
