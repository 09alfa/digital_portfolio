# P02 | Managed Services in Customer-Owned Cloud

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Global technology reseller, systems integrator and Azure Expert MSP (UK) |
| **My role** | Product Manager, Cloud and Managed Services |
| **Deployment model** | Managed services running inside customers' own AWS and Azure accounts |
| **Portfolio** | Hybrid cloud, AWS and Azure managed services, backup and resilience, database and operating system services, monitoring, modern workspace, packaged professional services |
| **Focus** | Portfolio ownership, service boundaries, pricing, launch, transition |

## Context

The managed services portfolio had grown offer by offer. Service definitions were inconsistent, customers struggled to see what was included, and new services took too long to move from sale into support.

Most services ran in the customer's own cloud environment. Depending on contract, the customer paid the cloud provider directly or was billed through us, so every offer had to be clear about who owned what.

## What I did

- **Defined the portfolio.** Brought 20+ service categories and 50+ specialised consulting deliverables, such as health checks and IT assessments, into repeatable, supportable offers.
- **Set service boundaries.** For each offer, decided which capabilities were included, which were phased for later and which were left out, based on what could be supported reliably in a customer-owned environment.
- **Reworked pricing and packaging.** Rebuilt service tiers across consumption-based and managed models so customers could choose the level of support that matched their environment.
- **Prioritised with evidence.** Ranked hundreds of customer feedback items by value, engineering effort, risk and operational readiness to set roadmap order.
- **Took offers to market.** Worked with product marketing on messaging, competitive analysis, webinars, references and proposals, and briefed vendor partners and the sales field on deployment options.
- **Protected customer commitments.** Partnered with security, compliance and legal so that service specifications matched what customers had been promised.
- **Built in monitoring.** Designed monitoring (ScienceLogic) into managed services so customers had availability and performance visibility after go-live.
- **Used AI for ideation.** Used Microsoft Copilot and Aha! AI features to group and rank ideas by impact and effort, then Power BI what-if models to test commercial scenarios before business-case approval.
- **Held the line at release.** Stopped premature releases at readiness reviews when commercial pressure had hidden open risks.

## Results

- **20+ new and revamped products** launched
- **A million USD** added to the sales pipeline from repositioned offers (pipeline, not realised revenue)
- **30%** faster service transition (see [D05](../delivery/05-service-transition-onboarding.md))
- **11** redundant onboarding steps removed; time-to-value cut from days to hours
- Repeated critical post-launch escalations resolved by aligning support capability before launch
- OKR and KPI dashboards that sped up executive investment approval

## What this shows

- Portfolio product management in a global matrix organisation
- Deciding what goes in, what waits and what stays out for a customer-owned deployment model
- Working on the partner, reseller and systems integrator side of the cloud market
- Commercial ownership through pricing, packaging and pipeline

**Tools:** Aha!, Power BI, Microsoft Copilot, ScienceLogic, Salesforce, Jira
