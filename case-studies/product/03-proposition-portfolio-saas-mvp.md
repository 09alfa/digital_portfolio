# P03 | Proposition Portfolio and B2B SaaS MVP

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | UK product and technology consultancy |
| **My role** | Product Manager (product, proposition and business operations) |
| **Team** | Four people across three countries, fully remote |
| **Product** | B2B SaaS for small logistics businesses, plus a portfolio of service propositions |
| **Focus** | Discovery, deciding what should exist, MVP scope, KPIs, budget |

## Context

An early-stage business with limited budget needed to decide which products and services were worth building, then prove demand quickly for its first SaaS product.

## What I did

**Proposition portfolio**
- Ran discovery with 50+ customers across 20 industry sectors.
- Turned the findings into five B2B propositions, each with a defined target segment, positioning, messaging, pricing, packaging and service tiers.
- Set go/no-go criteria so investment followed evidence rather than enthusiasm.

**Logistics SaaS MVP**
- Narrowed discovery to four priority pain points for small logistics businesses, centred on reliable delivery planning and faster change.
- Converted those pain points into MVP scope, a prioritised backlog, acceptance criteria and a release plan.
- Used AI-assisted prototyping (ChatGPT for research synthesis, Uizard for clickable concepts) to test ideas with customers before committing engineering time.
- Balanced customer timelines against the capacity of a small distributed team, trading scope rather than quality.

**Measurement**
- Set four product and business KPIs covering progress, customer and commercial performance, and delivery health.

**Feedback process for a client**
- For a technology company client, designed a structured feedback ingestion process that pulled input from several channels into one prioritised backlog.

## Results

- **21** beta-stage customers secured
- **2** early activations, validating product value
- **15%** under the approved budget
- **5** propositions defined and ready for market

## What this shows

- Deciding what products should exist, not only when they ship
- Lean discovery and MVP definition on a tight budget
- AI-assisted research and prototyping as daily practice
- Leading a remote team without line authority

**Tools:** Miro, ChatGPT, Uizard, Jira, Aha!, Power BI
