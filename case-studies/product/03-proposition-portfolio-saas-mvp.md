# P03 | Proposition Portfolio and B2B SaaS MVP

> Used customer discovery to decide which products were worth building, then took a logistics SaaS MVP to 21 beta customers while coming in 15% under budget.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | UK product and technology consultancy |
| **My role** | Product Manager (product, proposition and business operations) |
| **Period** | 2024 to present |
| **Team** | Four people across three countries, fully remote |
| **Relevant for** | Product Manager, Product and Proposition Manager, Product Owner, Delivery Manager |

## Context

An early-stage business with limited budget needed to decide which products and services to build, then prove demand quickly for its first SaaS product.

## Challenge

Choose well with little money and a small distributed team, and avoid building things customers would not pay for.

## What I did

**Proposition portfolio**
- Ran discovery with 50+ customers across 20 industry sectors.
- Turned the findings into five B2B propositions, each with a target segment, positioning, messaging, pricing, packaging and service tiers.
- Set go/no-go criteria so investment followed evidence.

**Logistics SaaS MVP**
- Narrowed discovery to four priority pain points for small logistics businesses, centred on reliable delivery planning and faster change.
- Converted them into MVP scope, a prioritised backlog, acceptance criteria and a release plan.
- Used AI-assisted prototyping (ChatGPT for research synthesis, Uizard for clickable concepts) to test ideas with customers before committing engineering time.
- Balanced customer timelines against team capacity, trading scope rather than quality.

**Measurement**
- Set four product and business KPIs covering progress, customer and commercial performance, and delivery health.

**Client work**
- For a technology company client, designed a structured feedback ingestion process that pulled input from several channels into one prioritised backlog.

## Results

- **21** beta-stage customers
- **2** early activations, validating product value
- **15%** under the approved budget
- **5** propositions ready for market

## Skills demonstrated

Customer discovery; deciding what products should exist; MVP definition; backlog management; KPIs; budget control; AI-assisted research and prototyping; leading a remote team without line authority.

## Methods and tools

Miro, ChatGPT, Uizard, Jira, Aha!, Power BI; jobs to be done, MoSCoW, Business Model Canvas.
