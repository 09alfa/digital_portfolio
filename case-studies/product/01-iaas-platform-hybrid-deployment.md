# P01 | IaaS Platform with Hosted and Customer-Premises Deployment

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Listed West African IT services group with a Tier-3 data centre |
| **My role** | Senior Product Manager (initiated and owned the platform) |
| **Deployment models** | Hosted in the provider's data centre; self-hosted private cloud on customer premises; in-country data residency |
| **Customers** | Banks, insurers, oil and gas, manufacturing, SMBs and technology hubs |
| **Focus** | Product strategy, deployment models, pricing and packaging, go-to-market |

## Context

Enterprise customers, especially regulated ones, were paying heavily upfront for infrastructure that sat partly idle. Buying cycles were long, and many could not move workloads to overseas public cloud because of data protection rules.

## The product problem

How do we give these customers cloud economics without asking them to give up control of where their data lives, or to rebuild how they buy technology?

## What I did

- **Validated demand before investing.** Ran discovery with prospective customers across sectors to confirm which workloads they would move first: test and development, disaster recovery, backup and production.
- **Designed two deployment models on one platform.** Customers could consume capacity hosted in our data centre, or run a dedicated private cloud on their own premises. Both kept data in-country.
- **Decided what each model would include.** Compute, storage, backup and disaster recovery as a service were available in both. Partner PaaS and SaaS services, such as data archiving and analytics, were offered where the hosting model supported them.
- **Built a consumption pricing model.** Compute priced per virtual server (memory, vCPU and disk), storage per terabyte with a minimum commitment, basic administration as a percentage of hosting, specialist administration by service agreement, and revenue share with platform partners.
- **Shaped the go-to-market.** Positioned the offer around moving from capital to operating spend, pay as you grow, cost visibility and in-country hosting. Ran it to market through proposals, customer references and webinars, with enablement for sales, pre-sales and technical teams.
- **Covered mixed software estates.** The platform hosted Linux, open-source and commercial software stacks, so customers did not need to change application vendors to adopt it.

## Results

- **19%** reduction in operating costs through platform modernisation
- A **new recurring revenue** stream for the business
- **70%+** reduction in technology-acquisition costs for four financial institutions
- A repeatable base for later IaaS and SaaS services (see [P04](04-xaas-platform-scale-reliability.md))

## What this shows

- Owning a platform product from idea to in-life service
- Understanding how deployment choice changes the product, its operations and its economics
- Pricing and packaging across deployment models
- Turning infrastructure capability into a commercial proposition

**Methods:** customer discovery, value proposition canvas, pricing models, business cases, go-to-market planning
