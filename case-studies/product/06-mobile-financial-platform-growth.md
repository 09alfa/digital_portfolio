# P06 | Mobile Financial Platform Growth

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Listed West African IT services group, in partnership with a bank and a mobile network operator |
| **My role** | Business Analyst, then product lead |
| **Channel** | USSD and mobile, supported by an agent network |
| **Focus** | Data-led prioritisation, journey redesign, partner management, adoption |

## Context

A mobile financial service had strong demand but lost most users partway through the USSD journey. Around 80% of sessions were dropping before completion.

## What I did

- **Found where users dropped out.** Mapped the end-to-end journey and analysed user interviews, agent feedback and 100,000+ feedback data points to find the steps with the highest drop-off.
- **Redesigned the journey with partners.** Worked with the telecoms operator and value-added service partners to shorten and simplify the USSD flow.
- **Prioritised with OKRs.** Used OKR-led roadmap prioritisation so the team worked on the changes with the most impact on completed transactions.
- **Tested before scaling.** Guided UX and engineering teams in prototyping and A/B testing new features before full release.

## Results

- Active users grew from about **500,000 to over 3m**, roughly six-fold in four years
- USSD session drop-off fell from about **80% to under 15%**
- Transaction volume rose by more than **300%**
- New-feature adoption rose by **35%** within a year

## What this shows

- Using data to decide what to fix first
- Managing external partners whose systems shape the customer experience
- Growth as a product problem: activation, completion and repeat use
