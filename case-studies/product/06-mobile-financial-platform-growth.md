# P06 | Mobile Platform Growth Through Data-Led Prioritisation

> Found where users were dropping out, fixed it with partners, and grew active users six-fold.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Listed West African IT services group, with a bank and a mobile network operator |
| **My role** | Business Analyst, then product lead |
| **Period** | Within 2015 to 2023 |
| **Channel** | USSD and mobile, supported by an agent network |
| **Relevant for** | Product Manager, Senior Product Manager, Business Analyst |

## Context

A mobile financial service had strong demand but lost most users partway through the USSD journey. About 80% of sessions dropped before completion.

## Challenge

Work out which fixes would matter most, when the journey depended on systems owned by external partners.

## What I did

- **Found where users dropped out.** Mapped the end-to-end journey and analysed user interviews, agent feedback and 100,000+ feedback data points.
- **Redesigned the journey with partners.** Worked with the telecoms operator and value-added service partners to shorten and simplify the flow.
- **Prioritised with OKRs** so the team worked on the changes with most impact on completed transactions.
- **Tested before scaling.** Guided UX and engineering teams in prototyping and A/B testing new features.

## Results

- Active users grew from about **500,000 to over 3m**, roughly six-fold in four years
- Session drop-off fell from about **80% to under 15%**
- Transaction volume rose by more than **300%**
- New-feature adoption rose by **35%** within a year

## Skills demonstrated

Data analysis; journey mapping; partner management; OKR-led prioritisation; experimentation; growth as a product problem.

## Methods and tools

Journey mapping, A/B testing, OKRs, user interviews.
