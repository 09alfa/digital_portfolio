# P02 | Managed Services Portfolio and Productisation

> Turned an inconsistent set of cloud and managed services into a clear, priced and supportable portfolio, launching 20+ products and adding a million USD to sales pipeline.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Global technology reseller, systems integrator and Azure Expert MSP (UK) |
| **My role** | Product Manager, Cloud and Managed Services |
| **Period** | 2023 to 2024 |
| **Portfolio** | Hybrid cloud, AWS and Azure managed services, backup and resilience, database and operating system services, monitoring, modern workspace, packaged professional services |
| **Relevant for** | Product Manager, Product and Proposition Manager, Service Delivery Manager, IT Manager, Technical Account Manager |

## Context

The managed services portfolio had grown offer by offer. Service definitions were inconsistent, customers struggled to see what was included, and new services took too long to move from sale into support.

## Challenge

Make the portfolio easy to buy, sell and support, inside a global matrix organisation where sales, technical, vendor, legal, marketing and operations teams all had a say.

## What I did

- **Defined the portfolio.** Brought 20+ service categories and 50+ specialised consulting deliverables, such as health checks and IT assessments, into repeatable, supportable offers.
- **Set clear service boundaries.** For each offer, defined what was included, what would follow later and what was out of scope. Many services ran in the customer's own AWS or Azure environment, so ownership and billing (direct to the cloud provider or through us, depending on contract) had to be explicit.
- **Reworked pricing and packaging.** Rebuilt service tiers across consumption-based and managed models.
- **Prioritised with evidence.** Ranked hundreds of customer feedback items by value, effort, risk and operational readiness to set roadmap order.
- **Wrote business cases** that secured investment for new and repackaged services.
- **Took offers to market.** Worked with product marketing on messaging, competitive analysis, webinars, references and proposals, and briefed vendor partners and the sales team.
- **Protected customer commitments.** Partnered with security, compliance and legal so service specifications matched what customers had been promised.
- **Built in monitoring** (ScienceLogic) so customers had availability and performance visibility after go-live.
- **Used AI for ideation.** Microsoft Copilot and Aha! AI features to group and rank ideas by impact and effort; Power BI what-if models to test commercial scenarios before business-case approval.

## Results

- **20+** new and revamped products launched
- **A million USD** added to sales pipeline from repositioned offers (pipeline, not realised revenue)
- **30%** faster service transition (see [D05](../delivery/05-service-transition-onboarding.md))
- OKR and KPI dashboards that sped up executive investment approval

## Skills demonstrated

Portfolio management; productisation; pricing and packaging; business cases; stakeholder management across a global matrix; vendor and partner engagement; AI-assisted product practice.

## Methods and tools

Aha!, Power BI, Microsoft Copilot, ScienceLogic, Salesforce, Jira; RICE prioritisation, OKRs.
