# How I Work

Principles drawn from the work in this portfolio. Each links to a place where it made a difference.

**1. Evidence before investment.**
I validate demand before money is committed: discovery with customers, then a business case, then build. The IaaS platform ([P01](../case-studies/product/01-iaas-platform.md)) and the proposition portfolio ([P03](../case-studies/product/03-proposition-portfolio-saas-mvp.md)) both started this way.

**2. Fix the biggest problem first, and know which one it is.**
Data tells me where customers are struggling. On the mobile platform ([P06](../case-studies/product/06-mobile-financial-platform-growth.md)), journey analysis showed exactly where 80% of users were leaving.

**3. Agree boundaries before go-live, not after.**
Who owns what, what is in scope and what support looks like should be settled before the first customer arrives ([P02](../case-studies/product/02-managed-services-portfolio.md), [P05](../case-studies/product/05-devops-as-a-service.md)).

**4. Go/no-go on evidence.**
Readiness gates protect customers and teams. I will stop a release when the evidence is not there, even under commercial pressure ([D05](../case-studies/delivery/05-service-transition-onboarding.md)).

**5. Rehearse what can hurt you.**
Cutover, rollback and migration steps get rehearsed before they go live ([D01](../case-studies/delivery/01-banking-account-migration.md), [D04](../case-studies/delivery/04-multi-country-cloud-migrations.md)).

**6. Reuse before bespoke.**
A repeatable model beats a hundred one-off projects. Repeated requests become product features ([D02](../case-studies/delivery/02-api-integration-programme.md)).

**7. Measure outcomes, not effort.**
Where I can, I move engagements from counting people and hours to agreeing outcomes ([D03](../case-studies/delivery/03-core-banking-process-automation.md)).

**8. Influence through relationships, not escalation.**
When stakeholders go quiet, I find allies who can help before reaching for the escalation route ([D03](../case-studies/delivery/03-core-banking-process-automation.md)).

**9. Keep project and business-as-usual apart.**
Separate teams, with regular checks on how the project affects live service.
