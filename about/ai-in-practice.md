# AI in My Practice

I use AI tools to speed up research, analysis and prototyping, then apply judgement to the output. I hold the IBM AI Product Manager certification.

## Current practice (2024 to present)

| Activity | Tools | How I use them |
|---|---|---|
| Market and customer research | ChatGPT | Summarise market information and synthesise discovery notes into themes |
| Rapid prototyping | Uizard | Turn concepts into clickable screens customers can react to before engineering starts |
| Discovery workshops | Miro | Structure and cluster research with stakeholders |
| Delivery planning | AI assistants | Draft plans and options for review and refinement |

See [P03](../case-studies/product/03-proposition-portfolio-saas-mvp.md).

## Enterprise practice (2023 to 2024)

| Activity | Tools | How I used them |
|---|---|---|
| Early product ideation | Microsoft Copilot, Aha! AI features | Group customer ideas into concepts and rank them by impact and effort |
| Commercial scenarios | Power BI what-if analysis | Test pricing and demand scenarios before committing roadmap and business-case investment |

See [P02](../case-studies/product/02-managed-services-portfolio.md).

## My rules for using AI

- AI drafts; I decide. Every output is checked against evidence before it shapes a decision.
- Customer data and confidential material stay out of public tools.
- Prototypes are for learning, not for promising features.
